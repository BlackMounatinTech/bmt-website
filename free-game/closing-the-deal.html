Closing the deal

Answer their questions and confirm the agreement.

  1. Go through the quote

    Make sure the customer understands what is included and answer their questions.

  2. Follow up

    Agree on a useful time to check in. Find out whether anything is preventing a decision.

  3. Confirm acceptance

    Get the agreed scope and terms accepted in writing. Explain the payment and scheduling steps before work begins.
