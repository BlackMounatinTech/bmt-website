Quoting and contract

Put the scope, price, and terms in writing.

  1. Build the price

    Account for labour, materials, equipment, disposal, overhead, and profit.

  2. Write the scope

    Describe the work included, exclusions, and assumptions. Make clear what the price covers.

  3. Set out the agreement

    Document payment terms, scheduling, responsibilities, and how changes will be approved. Use a contract suitable for the work and location.
