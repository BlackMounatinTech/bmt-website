Onsite consultation

See the project, ask questions, and agree on what needs doing.

  1. Walk through the job

    Let the customer show you the problem and explain what they want.

  2. Gather the details

    Measure, take photos with permission, and check access, existing conditions, and anything that could affect the work.

  3. Confirm the scope

    Repeat back what you understand. Discuss options and explain when they can expect the quote.
