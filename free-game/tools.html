Job pricing calculator

Add your estimated costs and choose a target margin. Use one currency throughout. The result is a price before sales tax.

Enter your costs, then calculate.

Margin is different from markup. A 30% margin means costs take up 70% of the selling price. Formula: price = costs ÷ (1 − margin). Missing costs make the margin look better than it is. This is an estimate, not a complete profit statement.

Quote results calculator

Choose one group of quotes—for example, all quotes you sent in a particular month. Count where those same quotes stand today.

Enter your quote counts, then calculate.

Accepted ÷ all sent includes undecided quotes. Accepted ÷ decided quotes excludes them. Neither number predicts whether open quotes will turn into work.
