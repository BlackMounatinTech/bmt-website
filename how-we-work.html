02 / GUIDANCE AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Monthly coaching.

For owners who want a clear direction and someone to work through decisions with. You or your team carry out the work, with guidance and feedback from Michael.

Foundation

Choose one main priority, build a practical action plan, and review your progress through regular coaching sessions.

  • Clear next steps for your business
  • Feedback on your marketing and sales process
  • Simple progress tracking and accountability

Growth

More frequent support as you improve how you attract customers, quote work and run the business.

  • Weekly coaching and progress reviews
  • Deeper help with your offer, marketing and follow-up
  • Adjustments based on what is actually happening

These are monthly programs. We explain the meeting schedule, support, deliverables and cancellation terms before you join. Website builds, creative production and automation setup can be scoped separately when needed.

03 / BUILD THE PLAN TOGETHER

90-day coaching and implementation.

For contractors who want help doing the work as well as deciding what to do. Each package has a fixed total project price and an agreed scope covering the full 90 days.

Coaching + Implementation

Focus on a defined set of improvements. We combine coaching with agreed deliverables, while you and your team handle your part of the plan.

That could mean improving your business message, building a focused landing page, or creating campaign assets. We choose the work around your priority and confirm what is included.

Full Support

A broader plan that can bring coaching, marketing, website work and AI or automation together.

Everything in your agreed plan is included. We define the builds, content, support and revisions upfront, then sequence the work over the 90 days.

Before starting, you receive the total price, payment schedule, calls, deliverables, revision limits and responsibilities in writing. Larger builds have agreed milestones and depend on timely access, assets and approvals.

What we can help with.

We select the services that fit your problem. You do not need every tool or every service.

Marketing and brand

Your ideal customer, service offer, messaging, brand direction, ad creative and social media content.

Your online presence

Websites, landing pages and Google Business Profile improvements that explain your work and make contacting you straightforward.

Sales and business processes

Handling inquiries, quoting, following up, planning work and tracking a few useful business numbers.

AI and automation

Practical workflows that reduce repeated admin, connect your tools and support a process that already makes sense.

For monthly coaching and Coaching + Implementation, AI and automation builds can be separately scoped add-ons. Full Support can include them within the agreed plan. Ad spend, software, domains, hosting, messaging usage and on-site photo or video production are separate costs, explained before you commit.

How the 90 days unfold.

  1. Month 1: Understand and prepare

    Review the business, record a starting point, agree on priorities, and gather the access and materials needed. Begin the first improvements.

  2. Month 2: Build and put it into use

    Deliver the agreed work in stages, test it, and help your team use it. Review progress and resolve the practical issues together.

  3. Month 3: Refine and hand over

    Review what changed, improve the work, and document the next steps. Confirm any remaining milestones and discuss ongoing support if it would help.

The exact schedule depends on your scope. We commit to agreed work and milestones; business results also depend on your market, offer and follow-through.
