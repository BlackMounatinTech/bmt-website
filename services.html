WHAT’S INCLUDED

You receive these four parts in one project.

You provide your business details and project photos. I do the setup and design work. Your proposal confirms the price, schedule and two rounds of revisions before we begin.

I build a one-page website for your business.

  • I write and arrange the page to explain your services and the areas you serve.
  • I add your project photos and any customer reviews you supply.
  • I make the page work on phones and computers.
  • I add a phone link and an inquiry form so customers can ask about their job.
  • I set the page title and description for search engines.
  • I launch the approved website and give you the website files and a handover.

This gives you one link to share with customers so they can understand your business, see your work and contact you.

I prepare basic branding that you can keep using.

  • I clean up your existing logo for use on your website and profiles.
  • I choose colours and fonts that work together.
  • I provide your finished logo files and a short colour and font guide.
  • I apply that visual style to your website, profile images and starter posts.

This makes your business easier to recognise across your website and social pages. A new logo design can be quoted separately if you need one.

I set up your business profiles and create six social posts.

  • I create or update your Facebook and Instagram business profiles.
  • I add your business description, contact details, profile images and website link.
  • I create six finished post designs using your project photos and business details.
  • I write a caption for each post and give you the images and captions to publish.

This gives visitors clear business information and gives you six ready-to-use posts to show your work. Regular posting and inbox management can be agreed separately.

I help you set up or improve your Google Business Profile.

  • I check whether your business is eligible for a profile.
  • I help add your business category, service area, contact details, photos and website link.
  • I explain any verification steps you need to complete.

An approved profile can help people find your business in Google Search and Maps. Google controls eligibility, verification and approval.
