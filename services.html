FROM FIRST MESSAGE TO LAUNCH

Here’s how we get it done.

  1. Send your business

    Email your business name, service area, website or social page and what you want improved.

  2. Agree on the project

    Receive the scope, price, schedule and payment terms. You decide whether it fits.

  3. Review the work

    You supply business details and project photos. I prepare the work, then we use the agreed revision rounds.

  4. Launch & handover

    Approve the finished work, launch your website and receive your content files and account handover.

What you provide

Your business details, services, service area, contact details, existing logo and project photos you have permission to use. Customer reviews can be included when you supply them.

What we agree separately

Domain and hosting costs, extra pages, a new logo, filming, ad spend and ongoing management are itemized separately when needed. The launch package is a one-time project; ongoing support is optional.
