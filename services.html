Here is what I do and what your proposal includes.

I build websites that explain your business.

I create a website that shows your services, service area, project photos and contact details. Customers can understand what you do and request a quote.

Your proposal lists the pages, inquiry form, content, revision rounds, launch and handover. Domain and hosting costs are listed separately.

I create a consistent look and message for your business.

I help you explain what you offer and prepare the logo, colours, fonts and graphics you need for your website and marketing.

Your proposal states whether we are improving your existing brand or creating new designs, and lists the files and revisions you receive.

I create social media content that shows your work.

I turn your project photos and footage into posts, captions and short videos for your Facebook and Instagram pages. I can also set up or improve your business profiles.

Your proposal lists the platforms, number of posts and videos, posting responsibilities and delivery schedule. You provide the project photos and footage unless we agree on production separately.

I create and manage advertising for the services you want to promote.

We choose the service and area you want to advertise. I prepare the agreed ad copy, images or videos, set up the campaign and review its performance.

Your proposal lists the advertising platform, creative work, campaign management and reporting. Your advertising budget is separate from my fee and must be approved before spending starts.
