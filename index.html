A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Is this only for new businesses?

No. It also suits contractors with an outdated website or social pages that need a consistent look. We review what you already have first.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, services, service area, existing logo and project photos you have permission to use. I’ll send a short content checklist.

What does it cost?

I’ll send a fixed project quote after reviewing your business and the work needed. The proposal lists deliverables, timing, payment terms and any outside costs.

Will you run my social media afterward?

The package includes profile setup and six starter posts. Ongoing posting, advertising and website updates can be agreed separately after launch.

Will I own the website and accounts?

Your domain and business profiles stay in your name. We agree on hosting, account access and handover before starting.
