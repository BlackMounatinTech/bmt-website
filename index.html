Campaign evidence

An ad that opened the conversation.

20Messenger conversations started
$10.55Cost per conversation · CAD

For our Vancouver Island construction business, we ran a Facebook Messenger campaign. Someone seeing the ad could click through to send the company a message about the work they needed. Meta recorded 20 messaging conversations started from that campaign, with $211.06 CAD spent on the ads—an average of $10.55 per conversation.

That gave the business a place to ask about the project, find out where it was, and discuss arranging an estimate. Starting that conversation is the role of the ad; quoting and winning the job comes next.

Meta Ads Manager · January 1–November 26, 2026. The same campaign reached 7,987 people and recorded 110 link clicks. Conversations started is Meta’s reporting metric; it does not mean 20 qualified leads or booked jobs.
