A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Is this just for fence companies?

No. I work with contractors, including fencing, excavation, landscaping, concrete, and other local trades. The starting point is how your business currently gets and converts inquiries.

Do I need coaching to use the other services?

No. Coaching and implementation can be separate or combined. We agree on the support you want before starting.

Can you just build or improve my website?

Yes. A website can be a standalone project. We'll agree on the pages, content, contact process, price, and handover before work starts.

What would I pay monthly for?

An agreed coaching schedule, reviewing progress, or managing your website, campaigns, and follow-up systems. The monthly scope is defined up front. Advertising spend is separate from service fees.

Can you work with the software I already use?

We check your current setup first. For automated quote follow-up, we need a reliable way to know when a quote is sent and when it is accepted, declined, or answered. Compatibility is confirmed before we commit to an integration.

Do I need to book a Zoom call?

No. Send me your website and what you want help with by email. We can start there or have a short phone call.
