A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

Do we have to switch estimating software?

We start by checking your existing setup. Depending on the software, we may be able to connect it, use HighLevel estimates, or agree on a manual status update process. We confirm what's workable before you commit.

What happens when a customer replies or accepts?

We configure the relevant stop rules and notify your team. If a decision happens outside the connected system, your team needs to record it so the follow-up stays accurate.

Will it quote jobs or negotiate for us?

No. Your team sets prices, answers technical questions, and agrees on the work. This service manages follow-up on estimates you've already prepared.

What does it cost, and when can we start?

We confirm pricing and a start date after reviewing your quote volume, software, and messaging setup. Any required messaging registration and testing are included in the launch plan.

Is this a fit for my company?

It's designed for residential fence companies that send estimates regularly and still follow up manually. On the call, we'll look at your current process and whether there's a useful gap to fill.
